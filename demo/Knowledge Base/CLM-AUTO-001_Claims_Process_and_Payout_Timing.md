# Sterling Auto Insurance — Claims Process & Payout Timing

## Filing a claim
You can file an auto claim 24/7 in the Sterling app or by calling our claims line. When you file, have ready: the date and location of the loss, a description of what happened, photos of the damage, and a police report number if one was filed. Each claim is assigned a claim number and a claims adjuster.

## The review stages
Every claim moves through the same stages:
1. **Filed** — the claim is logged and a claim number is issued.
2. **Adjuster assigned** — a claims adjuster takes ownership and reviews the facts of loss against your coverage.
3. **In review** — the adjuster gathers the repair estimate, photos, and any third‑party or police information, and confirms the loss is covered.
4. **Decision** — the claim is **approved** (a payout is calculated, less any deductible) or **denied** (with a written reason).
5. **Payment** — for approved claims, the payout is issued.

## How long an approved payout takes
Once a claim is **approved**, Sterling issues the payout within **5 business days**. The payment is sent by your chosen method — direct deposit to you, or paid directly to the repair shop if you used a Sterling-network shop. Glass-only claims handled through a network shop are typically paid directly to the shop. Your deductible is subtracted from the payout amount (for example, a $3,000 covered repair with a $500 deductible pays out $2,500).

## Checking the status of a claim
You can see every claim, its status, the approved amount, and the scheduled payout date in the Sterling app, or ask the Sterling assistant "what's the status of my claims?" Approved claims show the **scheduled payout date**; in-review claims show what the adjuster is still waiting on.

## How you can help move an in-review claim along
A claim in review usually waits on information. You can speed it up by:
- Uploading clear **photos** of all damage from multiple angles.
- Getting a **repair estimate** from a shop and sending it to your adjuster (or choosing a Sterling-network shop, which sends the estimate for you).
- Responding promptly to any adjuster request for documents, a recorded statement, or clarification.
- Providing the **police report number** and the other driver's information for collision claims.
Claims with a complete estimate and photos are typically decided faster. You cannot change the coverage decision itself, but a complete file removes the delays.
