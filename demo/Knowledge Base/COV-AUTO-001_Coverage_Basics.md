# Sterling Auto Insurance — Coverage Basics

## What a Sterling auto policy includes
A Sterling auto policy is built from several coverages. Your specific limits and deductibles are listed on your declarations page, viewable in the app.

## Liability coverage
- **Bodily Injury Liability** pays for injuries you cause to other people in an at-fault accident, up to your limit.
- **Property Damage Liability** pays for damage you cause to other people's property (their vehicle, a fence, a building).
Liability is required in most states and does not pay for your own vehicle or injuries.

## Collision coverage
Collision pays to repair or replace your vehicle after a collision with another vehicle or object (a guardrail, a pole), regardless of fault, up to the vehicle's value and minus your deductible.

## Comprehensive coverage
Comprehensive pays for damage to your vehicle from non-collision causes: theft, vandalism, fire, hail and storms, falling objects, flooding, and animal strikes. **Glass/windshield** claims fall under comprehensive. It is subject to your deductible (glass claims may have a reduced or waived glass deductible depending on your policy).

## Uninsured / Underinsured Motorist
Pays for your injuries and, in some states, your vehicle damage when an at-fault driver has no insurance or not enough.

## Medical Payments
Pays reasonable medical expenses for you and your passengers after an accident, regardless of fault, up to the limit.

## Deductibles
A deductible is the amount you pay out of pocket before Sterling pays the rest on a collision or comprehensive claim. A higher deductible lowers your premium. Liability coverages have no deductible.

## Adding or changing coverage
You can add coverage (for example, raise a limit or add a glass rider) at any time. Coverage changes are **forward-looking**: they apply to losses that happen on or after the change's effective date. They do not apply retroactively to a loss that already occurred.
