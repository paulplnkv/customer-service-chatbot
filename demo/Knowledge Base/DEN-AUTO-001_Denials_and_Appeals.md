# Sterling Auto Insurance — Denials & Appeals

## Why a claim may be denied
Common reasons a claim is denied:
- **No coverage in force on the date of loss** — the policy was not yet effective, had expired, or had lapsed when the loss occurred.
- **The coverage needed was not carried** — for example, a single-vehicle collision on a liability-only policy with no collision coverage.
- **Excluded cause of loss** — wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, racing, or intentional damage.
- **The loss was not substantiated** — insufficient documentation of what happened or the damage.
Every denial comes with a written reason and the specific policy basis.

## Can renewing or adding coverage now reverse a denial?
No. Coverage must be **in force on the date of loss**. Renewing, reinstating, or adding coverage today is **forward-looking** and cannot retroactively cover a loss that already happened before that coverage existed. This is the most common follow-up question on a denied claim, and the answer is that a future-dated change does not change a past-dated loss.

## How to appeal or request a review
If you disagree with a denial, you can request a review. An appeal is the right path when you believe the facts behind the denial are wrong — for example:
- You have **proof the policy was actually in force** on the loss date (a payment confirmation, a binder, or a corrected effective date).
- The **date of loss is recorded incorrectly**.
- You have **additional evidence** (photos, a repair estimate, a police report, witness information) that wasn't considered.
Gather that documentation and ask a Sterling specialist to open a review. A specialist can look at your full record, explain exactly why the claim was denied, check whether an exception or endorsement applies, and tell you the documents that would change the outcome.

## When the assistant escalates to a specialist
For a denied claim — especially when a customer asks whether the decision can be changed, whether renewing would help, or wants to dispute the denial — the Sterling assistant connects you with a human claims specialist, who reviews your account and advises how to proceed.
