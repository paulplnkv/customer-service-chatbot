# Sterling Auto Insurance — Frequently Asked Questions

## When will my approved claim payment arrive?
Approved payouts are issued within 5 business days of approval. You can see the exact scheduled payout date for an approved claim in the app, or ask the assistant. The amount paid is the covered repair or loss amount minus your deductible. Payment goes to you by direct deposit, or directly to the repair shop if you used a Sterling-network shop.

## How do I check the status of my claims?
Open the Sterling app or ask the assistant "what's the status of my claims?" Each claim shows its status (in review, approved, denied, or paid), the amount, and — for approved claims — the scheduled payout date.

## My claim is still "in review." Can I do anything to speed it up?
Yes. A claim in review is usually waiting on information. Upload clear photos of the damage, send your repair estimate to the adjuster (or use a Sterling-network shop that submits it for you), and respond quickly to any adjuster requests. A complete file is decided faster. You can't change the coverage decision itself, but you can remove the delays.

## My claim was denied. What can I do?
Read the denial reason on the claim. If you believe the facts are wrong — for example the loss date is incorrect, or coverage actually was in force — you can request a review/appeal and provide supporting documents. A Sterling specialist can review your account and tell you the best path. See Denials & Appeals.

## My claim was denied because I wasn't covered yet. If I renew or add coverage now, will it get approved?
No. Coverage has to be in force on the date of the loss. Renewing, reinstating, or adding coverage now only applies to future losses — it cannot retroactively cover a loss that already happened before the coverage existed. If you think coverage actually was in force on the loss date, that's an appeal, and a specialist can investigate.

## How do I renew my policy?
Sterling sends a renewal offer before your expiration date. Renewing continues your coverage into the next 12-month term starting on the renewal date. You can review and renew in the app.

## How do I talk to a human?
Ask the assistant to connect you with a person, or it will offer to escalate when an issue needs a specialist (such as disputing a denied claim). A human agent reviews your account and follows up with a recommendation.
